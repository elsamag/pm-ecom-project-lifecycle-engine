## 1. Project Purpose & Executive Summary
The primary objective of the **E-Commerce PM Lifecycle Engine** is to eliminate chronic project delivery delays, scope creep, and role ambiguity across enterprise e-commerce initiatives. By implementing a structured 4-Phase Governance Framework (Initiation, Planning, Execution, Closing), this project secures budget control, enforces team accountability, and guarantees predictable deployment timelines.

---

## 2. Business Objectives & Core KPIs
* **On-Time Delivery Rate:** Achieve $\ge 98.4\%$ on-schedule phase completion.
* **Scope Variance:** Maintain zero unauthorized scope expansion via locked Phase Gates.
* **Budget Variance:** Target $0.0\%$ budget overrun across all sprint iterations.
* **Role Accountability:** Enforce $100\%$ task mapping using a verified RACI Matrix.

---

## 3. Project Scope

### In-Scope
* Deployment of the 4-Phase PM Lifecycle Engine (Initiation, Planning, Execution, Closing).
* Creation and maintenance of Work Breakdown Structures (WBS), RACI Matrices, and Risk Logs.
* Enforcement of strict Phase Gate approval checkpoints.
* Execution benchmarking and KPI status logging.

### Out-of-Scope
* Direct refactoring of legacy third-party vendor applications.
* Hardware procurement and physical infrastructure hosting.

## 5. Phase Gate Milestones

2. **Gate 2 (Planning):** WBS creation, RACI matrix approval, and risk register lock.
